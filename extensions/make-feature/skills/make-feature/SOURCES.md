# Sources and attribution

## Author methodology in this revision

Make Feature combines Artemiy Miller's six-stage problem-solving method (diagnosis, external research, alternatives and measurable choice, before/after states, anticipatory plan, honest challenge) with his Build Idea workflow: existing-code reconnaissance, a critic followed by an adjudicator, product premortem, phased implementation, integration review, and learning from observed failures. These are the author's methods, independently structured here; Make Feature does not claim to be a full replacement for Build Idea.

The local state helper and its original 33 tests come from the author's make-it-real commit `0282a33ccbe2c149dc4a9301b7d2f25152ff3bf1`. This revision adds feature-scoped metadata, actual-worker binding with recovery, resolved-target fingerprints, and preservation/link checks for Markdown context. Version0.3.0 adds adaptive quick/normal/deep routes, four canonical working documents, a shared durable delegation/repair/deadline budget, and compact receipt output. Additional tests cover those mechanisms; the helper is not an LLM executor or a token meter. Research and background-execution claims below retain their original limits; their presence is not a current execution benchmark.

Original Russian procedure and stdlib helper by Artemiy Miller, with Hermes Agent as writing/engineering collaborator. This is a synthesis of mechanisms, not a wrapper that installs or chains upstream frameworks. No upstream implementation, substantial prompt blocks or template text is bundled. MIT applies to this project's original material, not to third-party assets or services.

The design research supplied for this work was documentary, not a comparative benchmark or an execution of these frameworks. Pinned references below identify the reviewed snapshots; do not infer present-day API compatibility or superior success rates. Before adopting third-party code, recheck its exact license and preserve required notices. Ideas credited here do not license logos, fonts, paid blocks or datasets.
