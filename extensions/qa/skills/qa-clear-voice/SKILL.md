# Qa Clear Voice

Rewrite for brevity and unambiguous facts. Separate observed result, expected result, hypothesis, reproduction steps and impact. Preserve exact error messages, commands, values, identifiers and links; do not invent environment details. Avoid exaggerated certainty and generic AI phrasing. Keep each result independently verifiable, including time, build and context. This specializes the core editorial-naturalness skill for QA evidence; do not load both merely to restyle one sentence.

## Worked example and failure checks

Use the [task-specific field guide](references/FIELD_GUIDE.md) when the case involves multiple states, boundaries, missing evidence or an independent handoff. Its scenarios are synthetic, not proof of executed tests. For a simple case, apply the procedure above and do not load extra material.
