# Sources and editorial provenance

Clear Voice is an independent editorial skill. It draws on common editorial principles and selected ideas from these public MIT-licensed projects:

- [human-writing](https://github.com/codemistake/human-writing) - natural-language writing, tone, fidelity
- [humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer) - identifying repetitive AI-generated writing patterns
- [stop-slop](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop), MIT, [revision `8da1f030185bdfe8471220585162991eaeb970e9`](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop/tree/8da1f030185bdfe8471220585162991eaeb970e9) - reference for diagnosing filler openings, exaggerated contrast, unsupported intensifiers and repetitive rhetorical structure. The additional Clear Voice rules and synthetic cases are independently worded; no original skill file or examples were imported.

The skill's rules and examples are maintained as a separate work. See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and `licenses/` for relevant attributions. Public profiles are intentionally application-neutral; there are no references to private or owner-operated projects. The synthetic checks are not proof of model quality or scientific factual correctness.
